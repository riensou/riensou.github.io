During my time at UC Berkeley, I studied in UC Jazz with Frank Martin, Ted Moore, Dann Zinn, and Kai Eckhardt. I also lead my own ensemble in my final year. In prior years, I took lessons with Daniel Lucca Parenti and Joan Cifarelli.
