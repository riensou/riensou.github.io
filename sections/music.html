I play jazz bass and piano, write and record my own music, and perform with a few groups in the Bay Area.

Check out my personal artist Spotify, Frankly Martini on Spotify and Instagram, and The Fabric on Instagram.

During my time at UC Berkeley, I've studied in UC Jazz with Frank Martin, Ted Moore, and Dan Zinn. Prior to this, I've taken private lessons on bass with Daniel Lucca Parenti and on piano with Joan Cifarelli. I teach private lessons and lead my own ensemble in the UC Jazz program.
