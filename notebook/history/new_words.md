A list of new words discovered during reading

- A <u>geranium</u> is a popular flowering garden plant, a traditional symbol of friendship and protection, and a vivid red color.
- A <u>pyre</u> is a large pile of wood built to burn a dead body during a funeral rite.
- <u>Spasmodic</u> means happening suddenly, in brief bursts, and without regularity
- <u>Sardonic</u> means characterized by bitter, scornful, or cynical mocking
- A <u>proconsul</u> is a contemporary government official, colonial administrator, or commissioner given sweeping, semi-autonomous powers over a region or occupied territory.
- A <u>millenarian</u> is a person or belief system focused on a coming, radical transformation of society that brings in a new age of peace, justice, or perfection, often lasting a thousand years
- <u>Eschatological</u> is an adjective that describes things relating to eschatology, the part of theology focused on death, judgment, and the final destiny of the soul and humankind
- <u>Ferocity</u> is a noun that means the state of being extremely fierce, wild, violent, or intense
- <u>Paradigmatic</u> means serving as a typical example, model, or standard of something
- <u>Privation</u> is a formal noun that means the lack or loss of the basic necessities and comforts of life, such as food, shelter, or freedom.
- <u>Incisively</u> is an adverb that means acting or speaking in a clear, sharp, and direct way that cuts through confusion to get straight to the core of an issue
